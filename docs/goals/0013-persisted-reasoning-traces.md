# 0013 — Explore controlled edits to persisted reasoning traces

**Status:** Provisional — refine before activation.  
**Prerequisite:** [0009 — Select which recorded context crosses a harness boundary](0009-selective-context.md). Independent of 0010–0012.

## Intended advance

Where a local, open or explicitly accessible agent harness **actually records a reasoning trace**, users may be able to inspect, select or transform that recorded text as a distinct kind of conversation content before continuation. The aim is finer control of historical context, not control of another model's hidden cognitive state.

## Material bounds

Do not infer access to private chain-of-thought that is not recorded and authorized. A modified trace cannot be represented as genuine reasoning newly performed by the target model. No claims of execution-state migration, internal model-state fidelity, or guaranteed improvement in downstream reasoning. Keep provenance and transformed/original distinctions explicit.

## Provisional acceptance basis

- A documented, eligible native source demonstrates a persisted trace that can lawfully and technically be examined without exposing private data or inaccessible model internals.
- A bounded transformation can retain or omit selected exposed trace material, with the target's actual native behavior and semantic loss made visible.
- The resulting target never falsely asserts that user-edited trace text was generated by the target model, and standard continuation remains unaffected.

## Refinement needed before activation

First establish a real use case, an eligible source/target format and the product benefit compared with simply preserving or omitting the trace. Determine whether any active transformation is technically appropriate; a carefully evidenced **do-not-implement** conclusion is a legitimate outcome of refinement. This is a research direction, not a committed feature.
